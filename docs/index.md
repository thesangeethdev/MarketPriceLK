# MarketPriceLK

Daily Sri Lankan market price reports from the Central Bank of Sri Lanka (CBSL).

## What is MarketPriceLK?

MarketPriceLK automatically fetches daily price reports published by CBSL, parses the PDF data, and serves it through a simple REST API.

## Daily Schedule

Reports are automatically fetched every day at **9:00 AM Sri Lanka time** (Asia/Colombo).
